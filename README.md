# CheckoutDM Review: Sell on Instagram DMs with Automated Checkout, Payments & Order Management

You post a reel. People comment "price?" and "size M available?". Your DMs fill up. You reply one by one, share a payment link, ask for the address, wait for a screenshot, and then somehow remember to update the order sheet.

By the time you reply, half of them have gone cold.

That is the exact problem **[CheckoutDM](https://checkoutdm.com)** was built to solve.

CheckoutDM is a platform that turns your Instagram DMs into a complete checkout flow. A customer messages your account, browses your catalog, picks a product and size, adds it to the cart, enters their address, and pays, all inside the chat. You get the order in your dashboard.

And it is not limited to Instagram. With the **Storefront**, you can take orders from anywhere on the internet, including WhatsApp, YouTube and Telegram.

👉 **Start free, forever: [checkoutdm.com](https://checkoutdm.com)**

## What is CheckoutDM?

CheckoutDM connects to your Instagram Business account through Instagram's official integration and runs the entire "DM to paid order" journey automatically. No website is required, and no copy-pasting of payment links.

Think of it as a shop assistant that lives in your Instagram inbox, works around the clock, and never forgets to ask for the delivery address.

**No transaction fees, ever.** Payments go straight to your own account. You only pay your plan, and your buyers never pay CheckoutDM anything.

## How It Works

1. **Connect your Instagram Business account** with the official Instagram login
2. **Add your products** with images, prices, sizes and stock
3. **Add your payment method** (Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal, UPI or bank transfer)
4. **Share your Instagram.** Customers DM you and the checkout runs automatically
5. **Or share your Storefront link** on WhatsApp, YouTube, Telegram or anywhere else, and buyers pay on the same page
6. **Track and ship orders** from your dashboard

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a website?**
No. Customers can browse and buy entirely through Instagram DMs. You also get a shareable Storefront link that works as your catalog and checkout page.

**Can I sell outside Instagram?**
Yes. Share your Storefront link on WhatsApp, YouTube, Telegram or anywhere else. Buyers can browse, apply a coupon and pay on the same page, without ever DMing you.

**Does it work with a personal Instagram account?**
You need an Instagram Business or Creator account connected through Instagram's official login.

**Which payment methods are supported?**
Razorpay, Stripe and PayPal for automatic payments, plus UPI, QR code and bank transfer for manual payments.

**Does CheckoutDM take a cut of my sales?**
No. There are no transaction fees. Payment processor fees apply as pass-through.

**Can I take over a conversation myself?**
Yes. You can take over any chat, any time, on Starter and above.

**Is there a free plan?**
Yes. Free forever, with no card required. Upgrade whenever you need more.
